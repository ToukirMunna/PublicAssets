## 📦 Available Language Packs

### 1. Bengali Offline Language Pack (`bangla_pack.db`)
- **Version**: `v1.0`
- **File Name**: `bangla_pack.db`
- **File Size**: ~21 MB
- **Schema**:
  - `bangla_translations` (`word_id` PK, `transliteration`, `definition_bn`, `pos_thesaurus`, `senses_bn_json`)
- **Coverage**: 48,736 headwords, multi-sense definitions, and POS thesaurus matrices.
- **Direct Download**: Available under [Releases](https://github.com/ToukirMunna/PublicAssets/releases/tag/lexicore-v1.0).
